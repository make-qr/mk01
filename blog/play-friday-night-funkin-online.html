How to play Friday Night Funkin

  1. Press arrow keys (or ZFGH) matching the scrolling notes.
  2. Stay on beat to keep your health up.
  3. Misses drain health — too many and you lose.
  4. Clear weeks in Story mode or jump into Freeplay.

Beginner tips

  1. Focus on the receptor, not the character animations.
  2. Downscroll vs upscroll — pick what your brain likes.
  3. Practice hard songs in sections.
  4. Lower lag in settings if notes feel late/early.
  5. Warm up on easier tracks first.

Controls

Arrow keys or A-S-W-D / Z-X-C-V depending on bind settings.
