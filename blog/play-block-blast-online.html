How to play Block Blast

  1. Drag a shape onto the board.
  2. Fill complete rows or columns to clear them.
  3. Plan ahead so the next three pieces still fit.
  4. Chase combos by clearing multiple lines in sequence.

Beginner tips

  1. Keep the center flexible — don't wall yourself in.
  2. Save awkward pieces for openings you create.
  3. Clear often; a packed board ends runs fast.
  4. Think one piece ahead when scores get high.
  5. Restart early if the opening board is already clogged.

Controls
