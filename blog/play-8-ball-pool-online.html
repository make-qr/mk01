How to play 8 Ball Pool

  1. Break from the kitchen, then claim solids or stripes.
  2. Aim with the cue; set power carefully.
  3. Pocket your suit, then the 8-ball in the called pocket (rules vary).
  4. Avoid scratching on the 8.

Beginner tips

  1. Leave an easy follow-up instead of a flashy low-percentage shot.
  2. Use softer power for thin cuts.
  3. Plan cue-ball position after the contact.
  4. Clear clusters early when you can.
  5. On mobile, zoom your aim if the UI allows.
