How to play Slope

  1. Open the Slope game page and press Play.
  2. Use A/D or Left/Right to steer the ball.
  3. Stay near the center of the track when speed ramps up.
  4. Lean into turns early — late corrections send you off the edge.

Beginner tips

  1. Warm up with a few short runs before chasing a high score.
  2. Watch the track ahead, not only the ball.
  3. Avoid red blocks; they end the run on contact.
  4. If you wobble, ease toward the middle instead of oversteering.
  5. Take a break when reactions slow — Slope punishes fatigue.

Controls

Keyboard: Left/Right or A/D. On mobile, tilt or on-screen controls if available.
