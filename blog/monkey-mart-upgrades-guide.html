How to play Monkey Mart Upgrades

  1. Earn coins by stocking shelves and serving customers.
  2. Open the upgrade/unlock panel when you have a surplus.
  3. Buy worker/helper upgrades before optional cosmetics.
  4. Unlock the next production booth when shelves stay full.

Beginner tips

  1. Rule of thumb: automation > new booth > capacity > cosmetics.
  2. If you still carry everything, hire helpers first.
  3. Don't unlock three booths you can't staff.
  4. Reinvest profits immediately after each upgrade.
  5. Check our early-game strategy guide for the first hour plan.
