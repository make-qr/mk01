How to play Wordle Unlimited

  1. Type a valid five-letter guess and submit.
  2. Green = right letter, right spot; yellow = wrong spot; gray = not in word.
  3. Use feedback to narrow the answer within six tries.
  4. Start a new puzzle anytime in Unlimited mode.

Beginner tips

  1. Open with a word rich in vowels (ADIEU, AUDIO, etc.).
  2. Avoid reusing gray letters.
  3. Park yellows in new positions.
  4. On turn 3–4, prioritize information if two greens are stuck.
  5. Keep a mental list of common endings: -ING, -ERS, -TED.

Controls

Keyboard letters + Enter. On mobile, use the on-screen keyboard.
