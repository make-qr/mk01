What is Tetris?

Stack falling tetrominoes, clear lines, and survive as the speed increases.

Why players like Tetris

  • The original endlessly replayable puzzle.
  • Skill expression through setups and T-spins (where supported).
  • Quick rounds or marathon mode.
  • Works on keyboard and many touch builds.

How to play Tetris

  1. Move pieces left/right and rotate into place.
  2. Soft drop or hard drop to lock pieces.
  3. Clear 1–4 lines; Tetrises (4 lines) score big.
  4. Keep the stack low as gravity increases.

Beginner tips

  1. Leave a well for I-pieces when hunting Tetrises.
  2. Don't nest holes under blocks you can't reach.
  3. Hold (if available) to save an I or awkward piece.
  4. Look at the next queue, not only the active piece.
  5. When panicked, clear singles to buy time.

Controls

Arrows to move/drop; up or X/Z to rotate (varies by build).
