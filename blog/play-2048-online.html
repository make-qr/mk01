How to play 2048

  1. Swipe or use arrow keys to slide all tiles.
  2. Equal tiles merge into the next power of two.
  3. Keep your highest tile in a corner.
  4. Build descending chains toward that corner.

Beginner tips

  1. Pick one corner strategy and stick to it.
  2. Avoid random swipes that break your snake.
  3. Don't merge big tiles too early if it creates holes.
  4. When the board clogs, clear small tiles first.
  5. Undo isn't always available — think one move ahead.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on desktop; swipe on mobile.
