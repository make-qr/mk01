How to play FNAF 1

  1. Check cameras to track animatronics.
  2. Close left/right doors when something is at the door.
  3. Conserve power — darkness ends the night badly.
  4. Listen for audio cues between camera checks.

Beginner tips

  1. Don't stare at cams forever; check doors often.
  2. Freddy is more dangerous in later nights — track him.
  3. If power is low, minimize door time.
  4. Learn each character's movement rules night by night.
  5. Play in a dark room for the full effect (optional!).

Controls
