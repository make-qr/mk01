How to play Flappy Bird

  1. Tap/click to flap upward.
  2. Release to fall with gravity.
  3. Thread the bird through each pipe gap.
  4. Score rises with every pipe you clear.

Beginner tips

  1. Find a steady tap rhythm instead of panic mashing.
  2. Aim for the center of each gap.
  3. Reset focus after a silly early death.
  4. Sit back slightly from the screen — less overcorrection.
  5. Short sessions beat tilting the phone in frustration.

Controls

Click, tap, or space to flap.
